WHAT WE OFFER

Our Services

Comprehensive dental care tailored to every member of your family — from routine check-ups to advanced cosmetic treatments.

🦷

General Dentistry

Oral exams, professional cleaning & scaling, tooth-colored fillings, and preventive fluoride treatments to keep your smile healthy.

⚡

Root Canal Treatment

Painless, single-sitting RCT using rotary endodontic instruments. We save your natural teeth and eliminate infection with care.

😁

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, smile designing, and tooth jewellery to give you that confident, radiant smile.

📏

Braces & Aligners

Metal braces, ceramic braces, and invisible clear aligners to correct crooked teeth and bite issues for all ages.

🔩

Dental Implants

Permanent titanium implants to replace missing teeth with natural-looking, long-lasting results.

🏥

Extractions & Surgery

Safe, painless wisdom tooth removal and minor oral surgical procedures performed with utmost precision.

👶

Kids' Dentistry

Child-friendly dental care — fluoride application, sealants, space maintainers, and cavity prevention in a fun, stress-free environment.

🪥

Gum Care

Deep scaling, root planing, gum surgery, and depigmentation treatment for healthy, pink gums and fresh breath.
