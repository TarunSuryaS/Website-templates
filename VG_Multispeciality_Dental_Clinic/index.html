ABOUT THE CLINIC

Kolathur's Premier
Dental Destination

V.G. Multispeciality Dental Clinic, situated at Kolathur near V4 Senthil Nagar Police Booth on the 200 feet road, opposite to Senthil Nagar HP Petrol Pump, has been serving the people of Chennai since 2011.

We are a premier dental clinic managed by Dr. V. Aravinth M.D.S. (Chief Dental Surgeon), a Gold Medalist from the Government Dental College and Hospital, Chennai. Our team of eminent specialists covers every branch of dental care.

Our motto is simple: promote preventive measures at the earliest. We don't just treat teeth — we preserve smiles for a lifetime.

01

Preventive Focus

We believe in catching problems early. Regular check-ups and early intervention save you from painful, costly treatments later.

02

Multi-Specialist Team

Not just one doctor — we have specialists in Orthodontics, Prosthodontics, Oral Surgery, Periodontics, and Pedodontics under one roof.

03

Modern Technology

Digital X-rays, advanced rotary endodontics, and surgical-grade sterilization ensure precision and safety in every procedure.

COMPREHENSIVE TREATMENTS

Our Dental Services

From routine check-ups to complex surgical procedures, our clinic is fully equipped to handle every aspect of oral healthcare.

🦷

General Dentistry

Comprehensive oral examination, professional cleaning & scaling, tooth-colored composite fillings, and preventive fluoride treatment.

⚡

Root Canal Treatment

Painless single-sitting RCT using advanced rotary instruments. We save severely decayed teeth and eliminate infection with precision.

🔩

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking titanium implants to replace missing teeth. Immediate loading options available for faster results.

😁

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile designing, porcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening, and tooth jewellery for a confident, radiant smile.

📏

Orthodontics

Metal braces, ceramic braces, and invisible clear aligners to correct misaligned teeth and bite issues for all age groups.

🏥

Oral Surgery

Impacted wisdom tooth extraction, cyst removal, and minor oral surgical procedures performed by experienced maxillofacial specialists.

👶

Pediatric Dentistry

Child-friendly dental care including pit & fissure sealants, fluoride application, space maintainers, and habit-breaking appliances.

🪥

Periodontics

Treatment for gum diseases — deep scaling, root planing, flap surgery, and gum depigmentation for healthy, pink gums.
